## ✦ About Me

I'm an **AI/ML Engineer & Upcoming Researcher** pursuing B.Tech in CSE (AI) at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham (2023–2027), with an IEEE-published paper in clinical ML at ICOCT 2025.
